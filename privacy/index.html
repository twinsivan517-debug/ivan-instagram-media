Ivan Content Studio - Privacy Policy

Ivan Content Studio is a private app with one user: the owner of the Instagram account @twinsivan. It publishes to that account and reads how its own posts perform.

What it accesses

  • The owner's own Instagram account: profile basics, posts, post insights (views, reach, likes, saves, shares), the comments on the owner's posts, and the owner's Instagram direct messages: the last twenty messages of each conversation, read to find the conversations the app itself started and to answer in them.

What it keeps

  • Publishing receipts and insights for the owner's own posts.
  • From comments on those posts: the date and the word used in a comment that asks for the link, to count asks. From direct messages: what the app itself sent, and whether the person it wrote to follows the account at that moment. Never a username, name, profile or message text of yours. People and conversations are kept as one-way codes that cannot be turned back into an account.

What it never does

  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data.
  • It does not access any other Instagram account.
  • It runs no ads and no tracking.

Deleting your data

If you commented on a post by @twinsivan and want anything this app kept about your comment deleted, send a direct message to @twinsivan on Instagram saying "delete my data". It is deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.
